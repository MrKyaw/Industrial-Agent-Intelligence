# AHU high supply-air temperature

A rising supply-air temperature at stable return-air temperature points to a
cooling or airflow problem in the air-handling unit, not necessarily a chiller
capacity problem.

## Checks

1. Verify the cooling-valve command and actual position.
2. Compare supply-air and return-air sensors against a handheld reference.
3. Check filter differential pressure and fan operation.
4. Confirm chilled-water flow and delta-T across the coil.
5. Review whether the affected chiller has a concurrent high-pressure condition.

If the chiller is also alarming, stabilise the plant first. Do not force the AHU
valve open or lower the air setpoint outside its configured range.
