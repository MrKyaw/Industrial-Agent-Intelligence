# Low delta-T syndrome

A low chilled-water delta-T at high load usually means the plant is moving more
water than the coils can use. It is not fixed by lowering the supply setpoint.

## Diagnostic sequence

1. Compare supply and return temperatures against calibrated references.
2. Inspect coil valves for leakage, hunting, or lost authority.
3. Verify flow balance and pump staging.
4. Check coil fouling and air-side bypass.
5. Trend delta-T after each change; one snapshot is not evidence of a fix.

## Interaction with condenser faults

A simultaneous low delta-T and high condenser pressure can share a heat-transfer
root cause. Use the condenser approach trend to separate the two before dispatching
a mechanical repair.
