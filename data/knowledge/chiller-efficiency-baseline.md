# Chiller efficiency baseline

Compare efficiency at similar load and condenser conditions. A single kW/ton
reading is weak evidence; a persistent shift with stable load is actionable.

## Reference envelope

- kW/ton above roughly 0.95 at this plant's design conditions warrants review.
- COP below 3.6 is outside the expected operating envelope at the current load.
- Delta-T below 4.0 degC at high load suggests low delta-T syndrome.

## Interpretation

Rising kW/ton with rising condenser approach points to heat-rejection degradation.
Rising kW/ton with normal approach and low delta-T points to water-side distribution
or coil issues. Always separate load effects from efficiency effects before
recommending a mechanical repair.
